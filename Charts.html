Assignment 1: Vega-Lite Multi-Plot Variation Study

Dataset Sourced: Montgomery County Commercial Liquor Sales API.

1. Baseline Plot (Basic Scatter)

Description: This baseline plot maps Retail Sales against Warehouse Sales across our drink supply data using a standard linear scale. It communicates a direct comparison between individual products sold via distinct distribution tracks, demonstrating that most inventory clusters tightly near low baseline volume endpoints.

2. Variation A: Adding Color Categorization

Description: In this variation, I retained the baseline spatial coordinates but mapped the categorical feature "Item Type" to the color encoding channel. This shifts communication from basic distribution tracking to product segment classification, highlighting that Beer and Wine account for massive warehouse bulk records while Spirits maintain a wider retail layout spread.

3. Variation B: Logarithmic Axis Scale Shift

Description: Here, I altered both the X and Y axes from linear scales to logarithmic scales. This stretches out the bottom quadrants where the massive volume of products sit compressed together. It allows viewers to smoothly zoom and isolate overlaps among lower-selling entries without dropping outliers or warping raw dataset metrics.

4. Variation C: Alternative Mark Type (Aggregated Bar)

Description: For the final variation, I swapped out the scatter geometry circles for aggregated bar shapes. Instead of evaluating individual products line-by-line, this plot calculates the average retail performance grouped purely by Item Type categories. It trades fine-grained asset tracking for an immediate, macroscopic group performance summary.
